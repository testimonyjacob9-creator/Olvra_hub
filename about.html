About Olvra

Olvra is a growing ecosystem of web tools built for one purpose: to remove one more thing you have to worry about. Not hype, not noise — just small, dependable systems that quietly work.

What Olvra builds

Olvra is home to tools like WoodPay, a fintech wallet built for everyday use; CGPA Engine, a grading calculator built around real academic systems; and SnapPull, a clean way to pull down social video content. More tools are always in progress.

Who's behind Olvra

Olvra Systems was founded by Testimony Inaweoghena Jacob, known as Oliver Wood — an independent developer based in Nigeria, building everything from a phone, one tool at a time.
